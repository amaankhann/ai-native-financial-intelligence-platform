# Scenario Catalog

Scenarios should be reusable, parameterized and auditable.

## Liquidity stress
Shock cash availability, unfunded commitments, market depth or redemption assumptions.

## Market drawdown
Apply price shocks and volatility changes to public-market exposures and propagate to portfolio risk.

## Rate shock
Change rate assumptions and propagate to valuation, credit, forecast and performance features.

## Credit deterioration
Increase leverage/default/covenant risk and activate Credit and Risk.

## Private valuation haircut
Reduce private valuation observations while increasing valuation uncertainty and propagate the effect through concentration and portfolio risk.

## Exit delay
Extend realization timelines, increasing liquidity pressure and potentially changing forecast/performance expectations.

Every scenario should specify: assumptions, scope, magnitude, horizon, transmission rules, activated engines, outputs and provenance.
